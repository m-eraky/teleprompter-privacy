Privacy Policy – Teleprompter & Remote

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Teleprompter & Remote (“the app”) is published by Meraky. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

Your scripts and documents

Scripts you type, and the text the app reads from PDF, Word or text files you open, are stored only on your device, inside the app’s private storage. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere. Deleting a script or uninstalling the app removes them.

Bluetooth

The app asks for the “Nearby devices” (Bluetooth connect) permission only to connect to a Bluetooth teleprompter remote that you have paired with your phone, and to receive its button presses. The app does not scan for other devices, does not use Bluetooth to determine your location, and does not store or transmit information about your devices.

Files

The app only reads files that you choose to open in it (with the import button, “Open with” or “Share”). It does not browse or access any other files on your device.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The app has no sign-in, contains no advertising, and includes no analytics or tracking tools. It works fully offline.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
